# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the stakeholders involved in the Resort Booking Management System project, understand their roles and interests, and define how they will participate throughout the project.

---

# Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role |
|-------------|------|
| Resort Owner | Project Sponsor and decision-maker |
| Reception Staff | Primary users of the booking system |
| Customers | End users who make bookings and enquiries |
| Housekeeping Staff | Use booking information to prepare rooms |
| Accountant | Uses payment and financial reports |
| System Administrator | Maintains the application and user accounts |
| Developer | Designs and develops the system |
| Business Analyst | Gathers and documents business requirements |

# Stakeholder Responsibilities

### Resort Owner
- Approves the project budget.
- Defines business goals.
- Approves major decisions.

### Reception Staff
- Manage bookings.
- Update customer information.
- Respond to customer enquiries.

### Customers
- Submit booking requests.
- Choose their preferred communication method.
- Receive booking updates.

### Housekeeping Staff
- Prepare rooms.
- Update room availability after cleaning.

### Accountant
- Record and reconcile payments.
- Review financial reports.

### System Administrator
- Manage user accounts.
- Maintain system availability.
- Perform backups.

### Developer
- Design and develop the application.
- Fix defects.
- Implement approved requirements.

### Business Analyst
- Gather requirements.
- Analyse business processes.
- Produce project documentation.
- Support stakeholder communication.

---

# Conclusion

Successful delivery of the Resort Booking Management System depends on effective collaboration between all stakeholders. Regular communication, clearly defined responsibilities, and active stakeholder involvement will help ensure that the final solution meets the operational needs of Oluhle Resort.
