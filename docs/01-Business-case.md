# Project Title

**Resort Booking Management System – Oluhle Resort**

---

# Background

Oluhle Resort is a small-to-medium hospitality business that currently manages reservations using WhatsApp, phone calls, email, and a paper booking register.

Although this manual process has served the business in the past, it has become increasingly difficult to manage as the number of guests has grown. Reception staff spend significant time checking room availability, recording bookings manually, responding to customer enquiries, and preparing reports.

These manual processes have resulted in booking conflicts, delayed responses, misplaced records, and limited reporting capabilities.

---

# Problem Statement

The current booking process is manual, time-consuming, and inefficient.

The main challenges include:

- Double bookings
- Paper records that can be lost or damaged
- Slow response times to customer enquiries
- No real-time room availability
- Manual reporting processes
- Limited visibility of booking information
- Poor customer experience

---

# Proposed Solution

Develop a web-based **Resort Booking Management System** that centralises all booking activities while allowing customers to communicate using their preferred communication method.

Customers will be able to:

- View available rooms
- Check room availability
- Submit booking requests online
- Submit general enquiries
- Choose their preferred communication method:
  - WhatsApp
  - Email

The system will use the selected communication method for:

- Booking confirmations
- Booking updates
- Check-in reminders
- Booking cancellation notifications
- Responses to customer enquiries

Reception staff will manage bookings, enquiries, rooms, and customer information through a secure administration dashboard.

---

# Business Objectives

The proposed system aims to:

- Eliminate paper-based booking records.
- Reduce double bookings by at least **95%**.
- Improve customer response times.
- Provide real-time room availability.
- Centralise customer information.
- Improve communication with guests.
- Generate automated business reports.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Enhance customer satisfaction.

---

# Project Scope

## In Scope

- Customer registration
- Online booking requests
- Room availability management
- Booking management
- Customer management
- Payment recording
- Check-in and check-out
- Reporting dashboard
- Staff authentication
- WhatsApp communication support
- Email communication support
- Customer enquiry management
- Customer communication preference selection

## Out of Scope

- Online payment gateway integration (future enhancement)
- Integration with third-party travel booking platforms
- Mobile application
- Loyalty programme

---

# Functional Requirements

The system shall:

- Allow customers to search for available rooms.
- Allow customers to submit booking requests.
- Allow customers to submit enquiries.
- Allow customers to choose their preferred communication method.
- Store customer communication preferences.
- Send booking confirmations.
- Send booking updates.
- Send booking cancellation notifications.
- Allow staff to manage bookings.
- Prevent double bookings.
- Generate business reports.
- Record customer payments.
- Maintain customer records.

---

# Assumptions

- Customers have access to the internet.
- Resort staff will receive training before implementation.
- WhatsApp and email services will be available for customer communication.
- Accurate room information will be maintained by staff.

---

# Constraints

- Budget limitations.
- Internet connectivity.
- Staff availability for training.
- Existing manual records will require migration.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- All bookings are managed digitally.
- Double bookings are reduced by at least **95%**.
- Room availability is updated automatically.
- Customer information is securely stored.
- Reports are generated automatically.
- Customers receive confirmations using their selected communication method.
- Staff manage enquiries through a single system.

---

# Business Value

The Resort Booking Management System will modernise Oluhle Resort's booking operations by replacing manual processes with a secure, centralised digital solution.

The system will improve operational efficiency, reduce administrative effort, enhance customer satisfaction, and provide accurate business information for decision-making.

By allowing customers to choose between **WhatsApp** and **email** for confirmations and enquiries, the system supports a flexible, customer-focused communication experience while providing a scalable foundation for future enhancements such as online payments and third-party booking platform integration.
