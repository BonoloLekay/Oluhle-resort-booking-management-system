# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to clearly define the business problems experienced by Oluhle Resort due to its current manual booking process and elaborate why a new booking management system is required.

---

# AS-IS

Oluhle Resort currently manages resevations using:

- Whatsapp
- Email
- Phone calls
- Paper booking register

Reception staff manually check room availability before confirming bookings.

Bookings are recorded in a paper register and customer communication is handled individually through Whatsapp or Email.

The process has become increasingly difficult as the number of customers has grown.

---

# Problem Description

The current booking process presents several operational challenges. These include:

- Double bookings
- Slow response times
- Manual room availability checks
- Paper records that may be lost or damaged
- Time-consuming reporting
- Difficulty tracking customer enquiries
- Inconsistent communication
- Limited visibility of business performance

---

# Root Causes

The challenges are mainly caused by:

- Manual booking procedures
- Lack of a centralized booking database
- Paper-based record keeping
- No automated availability checking
- Separate communication channels
- Manual reporting processes

---

# Business Impact

The current process negatively affects the business by:

- Reducing operational efficiency
- Increasing administrative workload
- Creating booking conflicts
- Delaying customer responses
- Limiting management reporting
- Reducing customer satisfaction
- Increasing the risk of lost business opportunities

---

# Desired Future State (TO-BE)

The proposed system will provide a centralized web-based booking management solution that enables customers to:

- View available rooms
- Submit booking requests online
- Submit enquiries
- Choose WhatsApp or email as their preferred communication method

Staff will be able to:

- Manage bookings
- Update room availability
- Manage customer information
- Record payments
- Generate reports
- Respond to enquiries from a single system

---

# Business Objectives

The new system aims to:

- Digitise the booking process
- Eliminate paper records
- Reduce booking conflicts
- Improve customer communication
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support business growth

---

# Success Measures

The project will be considered successful if:

- Double bookings are reduced by at least 95%.
- Customer response times improve.
- Reports are generated automatically.
- Staff manage bookings from one centralized system.
- Customers receive confirmations through their preferred communication method.

---

# Conclusion

The current manual booking process is no longer suitable for the operational needs of Oluhle Resort.

Implementing a web-based Resort Booking Management System will improve efficiency, reduce booking errors, enhance customer satisfaction, and provide a scalable platform to support the resort's future growth.
